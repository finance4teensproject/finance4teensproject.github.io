About Finance 4 Teens

Created by a student to make financial education more practical, accessible, and relevant for teens.

About the Project

Finance 4 Teens is my Girl Scout Gold Award project, created to help teenagers build practical financial skills and feel more confident making financial decisions. I created this project because topics like budgeting, saving, credit, debt, and college costs are important parts of everyday life, but many teens do not have many opportunities to learn about them in a practical and relatable way.

About Me

I'm Swati Seshadri, a student at Poolesville High School in the Science, Mathematics, and Computer Science Magnet Program. For my Girl Scout Gold Award, I wanted to combine my interests in finance and education to create something that could be useful to other students.

Through Finance 4 Teens, I created this website with educational lessons, interactive activities, financial tools, and downloadable resources. I am also working to provide financial literacy workshops for teens in my community.

My Goal

My goal is to make financial education easier to understand, easier to access, and more relevant to the real-life decisions teenagers will face. Finance 4 Teens is designed to give students a place to learn about money at their own pace and develop skills they can continue using as they get older.

What You'll Find Here

  • Lessons on budgeting, saving, credit, interest, debt, and college costs.
  • Interactive quizzes and activities to reinforce what you learn.
  • Downloadable worksheets and guides for practicing financial skills.
  • Financial tools and calculators for real-world situations.
  • Workshops and other opportunities to learn about personal finance.

About the Gold Award

The Girl Scout Gold Award is the highest award a Girl Scout can earn. It challenges Girl Scouts to identify a community issue, develop a sustainable solution, and lead a project that creates lasting impact.

Finance 4 Teens is my way of addressing the lack of accessible financial education for teenagers. Through the website, educational resources, and community programming, I hope to create resources that can continue helping students beyond the completion of my Gold Award project.
